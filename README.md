# Cluxe — Luxury Jewellery Website Concept

**A minimal, luxury-inspired jewellery website concept celebrating craftsmanship, elegance, and timeless design.**

🌐 **Live Demo:** [cluxe.vercel.app](https://cluxe.vercel.app/)

## 💎 Overview

Cluxe is a concept website designed to explore the visual identity and user experience of a premium handmade jewellery brand.

The project focuses on communicating elegance, exclusivity, and artisanal craftsmanship through a refined digital experience. Every design decision aims to complement the handcrafted nature of the jewellery while maintaining a calm, sophisticated, and timeless aesthetic.

**Industry:** Jewellery  
**Project Type:** Website Design & Development  
**Status:** Concept Project

## ✨ Design Approach

The design follows a minimal, luxury-inspired direction, emphasizing the product and its craftsmanship rather than unnecessary visual complexity.

Key design principles include:

- **Minimalism:** A clean, uncluttered layout that keeps the focus on the jewellery.
- **Elegant Typography:** Carefully selected typography to establish a refined visual identity.
- **Thoughtful Spacing:** Generous whitespace to create a sense of exclusivity and sophistication.
- **Soft Visual Elements:** Subtle design details that contribute to a calm, premium browsing experience.
- **User Experience:** An intuitive browsing experience that complements the brand's artisanal identity.

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Explore the visual direction of a premium handmade jewellery brand.
- Translate craftsmanship and exclusivity into a digital experience.
- Create a cohesive, elegant, and timeless website aesthetic.
- Demonstrate the role of typography, spacing, and visual hierarchy in luxury web design.
